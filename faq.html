The basics

What is GTA 6?

Grand Theft Auto VI is the sixth mainline entry in Rockstar Games' open-world crime series. It's a single-player action-adventure set in Leonida, a fictional Florida-like state anchored by Vice City, starring criminal couple Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — the series' first playable female protagonist. It launched November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, became the highest-rated game ever on Metacritic (99) and the biggest entertainment launch in history ($3.79B day one).

Sources: Rockstar · Take-Two · Metacritic

Is GTA 6 out? / Is GTA 6 released? / When can I play GTA 6?

Yes — it's out now worldwide since November 19, 2026, midnight local time, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. If you have one of those consoles, you can play it today. It is not available on PS4, PC, or natively on Xbox One.

Sources: PlayStation support · Rockstar

When did GTA 6 come out — release date and time?

November 19, 2026, rolling out at midnight local time across the world's time zones (PlayStation's official support page confirms "midnight local time"). Digital pre-orders could pre-load from November 12, 2026. Pre-orders opened June 25, 2026 at $79.99 / $99.99 Ultimate.

Sources: PlayStation support · Take-Two

How much is GTA 6? / How much is GTA 6 on PS5?

$79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate (digital only), identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in the US. UK: £69.99 / £89.99. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade (if you own Standard) is about $20 on the Xbox Store. See the full edition breakdown.

Sources: Take-Two · USA Today · GTA 6 Everything (price table)

What is GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

The $99.99 digital-only edition with premium extras threaded through the story: Jason's safehouse vehicles (Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak, Ganado Retro build), the Shitzu Squalo boat, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy + Paradise Garage, Vercetti Estate revolvers, personalized pistols, plus exclusive side content (the PTT Youngin$ compound raid and Wyman's Classic Car Collection missions).

Sources: PlayStation.com · Kotaku · Engadget

Platforms & PC

When will GTA 6 be on PC? / When does GTA 6 come out on PC?

Unknown — no PC version has been announced. A GamesBeat report claims Rockstar is targeting early 2027 for the PC port, but neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed a date, price, store or system requirements. Xbox also debunked launch-day PC streaming rumors. For context, GTA 5 hit PC about 19 months after its console launch.

Sources: VGC · The Independent · IBTimes UK

Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One? / GTA 6 Xbox One

Not natively — GTA 6 does not install or run on Xbox One. Xbox One players can stream it via Xbox Cloud Gaming (cloud playtime required). Native play is Xbox Series X|S only.

Sources: Xbox.com

GTA 6 on Xbox Series S — does it run well?

GTA 6 is available on Xbox Series X and Series S (native). For specific performance comparisons between Series S and Series X we haven't verified a reliable source yet, so we won't quote numbers — check Digital Foundry-style technical reviews before drawing conclusions.

Sources: Xbox.com

When is GTA 6 Online coming out? / GTA Online for GTA 6

No date announced. GTA 6 launched as a purely single-player game. Historically Rockstar ships multiplayer later (GTA Online: 2 weeks after GTA 5; Red Dead Online: months later). Speculation — a Bank of America note and launch-week chatter — points at a possible early 2027 window, but nothing is confirmed. If a date exists, it will appear first on the Rockstar Newswire.

Sources: The Independent · GameSpot · GamesRadar

Reviews & sales

GTA 6 Metacritic — what score did it get?

99/100 on both PS5 and Xbox Series X — "universal acclaim" from 150+ critic reviews, making it the highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic, ahead of Ocarina of Time (99, from just 22 reviews) and above GTA 5 and RDR2 (both 97). OpenCritic: 98. IGN: 10/10. The user score is a separate story: ~5.7 ("mixed or average", 120k+ ratings).

Sources: Metacritic · Polygon · GamesRadar

How much money has GTA 6 made so far? / GTA 6 sales

Officially confirmed: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold pre-release (Take-Two estimates through end of Nov 19, 2026) — the biggest entertainment launch in history across any medium, ~5× GTA 5's first day and past Avengers: Endgame's entire theatrical gross. Latest analyst estimate: Alinea Analytics put the launch weekend at 44.9 million copies and ~$4 billion gross (Nov 24, 2026). Total figures keep climbing; only Take-Two's future earnings reports will give the official cumulative number.

Sources: Take-Two · BBC · CNBC · Polygon — weekend estimates

Story, missions & map

How many chapters in GTA 6? How long is the story mode?

Five chapters + a prologue: Just Like Paradise → Paradise Lost → Tits Ass Drug Money → Vice City, Baby → Top of the World, Ma. About 91 main missions (counts of 93–96 include short story steps differently; the full in-game list exceeds 140 entries). Story length: ~50–80 hours; 90–100 with side content; 200+ for 100% (GameSpot). Tracked-player median: 56h 50m.

Sources: readdork · GameSpot · Who Finished

How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5? / mapa GTA 6

Officially: 2× the size of GTA 5, 3× Red Dead Redemption 2; Vice City is 2× Los Santos, and Vice City + suburbs is 11× Los Santos + suburbs (Rockstar's Rob Nelson via press previews). Fan-measured: Leonida's land is 82.7 km² vs GTA 5's 49.7 km² (≈1.7×; ~2× with navigable water; ~2.2× with the sea floor). Six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Mount Kalaga. Full breakdown on the map page.

Sources: Polygon · Mapheist · GTA Atlas / gtadb

How does GTA 6 end?

The finale is "The End of the Road" (mission 91). It ends at a police roadblock on a bridge with one decisive choice: Swim for It (Triangle/Y) or No Surrender (R2/RT). No Surrender ends with both protagonists dead (confirmed on footage); Swim for It has multiple possible outcomes depending on earlier choices, and guides disagree on whether 3, 4 or 5 endings exist. After the credits you return to free roam. Light-spoiler details on the story page.

Sources: lootester · GTA6-Help · everyblip

Characters

Who are the GTA 6 characters? / GTA 6 cast & voice actors

Playable: Jason Duval (Dylan Rourke) and Lucia Caminos (Manni L. Perez). Key supporting: Brian Heder (Stephen Root), Lori Heder (Orlagh Cassidy), Cal Hampton (Bobby Moynihan), Mandy (Lexie Shoaibi), Boobie Ike (Mike Forbs), Dre'Quan (Naiqui Macabroad), Raul Bautista (Manny Perez), Mia Caminos (Roselyn Sanchez), Donnie (Brad Carter). Michael Hollick (Niko Bellic's actor) is credited as "The Hitman". Full table on the characters page.

Sources: Polygon · GamesRadar · GameSpot

Who is Mandy in GTA 6? / mandy gta 6 / gta 6 mandy

Mandy is Brian Heder's tenant in the Leonida Keys — met in the Chapter 1 mission "Pipework" (the game's third mission) when Jason collects her rent. She flirts with Jason and rebuffs are mutual; the scene went viral for its horror-movie details (severed finger in the sink, acid tubs and a possible fresh grave in the garden — she may be a serial killer, though the game never confirms it). Played by Lexie Shoaibi, voice + motion capture, confirmed by the actress on Instagram after launch.

Sources: Vice · Wikipedia · indy100

Is Lucia trans in GTA 6?

No evidence supports it. The rumor began with a December 2023 viral tweet citing a sourceless Reddit thread. The finished game refers to Lucia as a woman throughout, places her in a women's-only prison in the prologue, and makes clear she hid her pregnancy from Jason. Rockstar has not commented on the speculation. Full breakdown on the characters page.

Sources: Game Rant · NPCWire · Sixth City Map

Is Niko Bellic in GTA 6? / niko bellic gta 6

Yes. GTA 4's protagonist appears as an older man in "Taking Liberties" (Chapter 5) — he confronts shipping boss Giorgio Gallo over a $2 billion cartel shipment, mentions he hasn't killed anyone since 2012, and leaves with a threat. Michael Hollick returns as his voice, credited as "The Hitman" to avoid spoiling it. Details on the characters page.

Sources: PC Gamer · esports.gg

Who is Manni L. Perez? / manni perez gta 6 / mann i perez

Manni L. Perez is the actress behind Lucia Caminos — one of GTA 6's two leads — confirmed by Rockstar's official launch credits. She'd been the subject of fan voice-identification speculation for years. (Related but distinct: Manny Perez is separately credited as Raul Bautista.)

Sources: GameSpot · AP via ABC News

Who is Dylan Rourke? / dylan rourke gta 6

Dylan Rourke is the actor who plays Jason Duval, GTA 6's male protagonist — voice and motion capture — confirmed in Rockstar's official credits at launch. Before launch, fans had spent years analyzing trailers trying to identify him.

Sources: Polygon · AP via ABC News

GTA 6 money cheat code — how does it work?

The money cheat gives $250,000 instantly. Code: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. On PS5: open the phone (D-pad Up), hold L1, press R1, then enter the arrows. On Xbox: same arrows, LB/RB instead. Unlock: finish the story + have made $250,000 from fenced goods. Caveat: it disables saving/trophies for the session — it's a toy, not an economy. Never trust "money generator" websites; they're scams.

Sources: Polygon · gta6codes (citing PowerPyx & Insider Gaming)
