GTA VI FAQ

This page is being rebuilt around claim-specific sources. It deliberately leaves out old claims about cheat inputs, scores, sales, missions and character details until they have been reviewed.

What platforms is Grand Theft Auto VI available on?

Rockstar’s official release and support pages state that Grand Theft Auto VI is available for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Check the official pages for regional release information and current platform availability.

Sources: Rockstar Games: Out Now · Rockstar Support

When did the current console release occur?

Rockstar Support states a November 19, 2026 release for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Availability can vary by region and store, so use the official support page for the current release information.

Source: Rockstar Support

Is there an officially confirmed PC release date?

OpenWorld Atlas has not completed a claim-specific review of a current official PC release announcement. We do not publish a PC date here until we can link directly to a relevant official source.

Are GTA VI cheat codes confirmed?

We have not completed source and testing review for individual cheat codes, inputs, unlock conditions, or game consequences. The old cheat list and money-cheat claims are being held for review. Do not rely on third-party code lists presented elsewhere as confirmation.

See the Cheat Code research page and Editorial Standards.

What are the Metacritic score, sales figures, story length and mission count?

These are distinct, time-sensitive claims. OpenWorld Atlas has not completed a source-by-source review for them, so this FAQ does not publish a number. A score, sales estimate, mission count or playtime will be added only with a directly relevant source and review date.

Which characters, locations, vehicles and missions are confirmed?

Each item needs its own source review. Promotional material may establish that a person or place appears, but it does not automatically establish a biography, voice actor, mission appearance, relationship, gameplay statistic or story outcome. Records are being reviewed before publication.
