# Editorial verification workflow

## Evidence and statuses

Evaluate each material claim independently. Prefer directly relevant official publisher, developer and platform sources for official announcements. Established journalism can support a `reported` classification when the article is inspected and its reporting basis is clear. Community research is a lead, not confirmation. Leaks, anonymous posts, search snippets and AI output are not publishable evidence by themselves.

Use these statuses consistently:

- `officially_confirmed`: directly supported by an official primary source.
- `independently_verified`: reproducibly tested or otherwise validated with documented method and evidence.
- `reported`: attributed to inspected, credible reporting but not officially confirmed or reproduced by this site.
- `unconfirmed`: a claim exists but available evidence is insufficient.
- `speculation`: an interpretation or prediction, clearly separated from factual records.
- `unknown`: no reliable information is available.
- `corrected/outdated`: retained for history but superseded or corrected.

Publication state (`draft`, `published`, etc.) is a separate editorial decision and must never be inferred from verification state.

## Claim review steps

1. Write the exact claim and identify whether it is time-sensitive.
2. Decide what evidence would establish that exact claim.
3. Open the source itself, inspect relevant content and dates, and check for corrections or newer information.
4. Record a direct source URL, source title, publication/update date, access date, claim supported, and any limitations.
5. Rewrite in original language that matches the evidence strength.
6. If evidence is inadequate, qualify, unpublish, or leave the field unknown. Do not fill missing numeric values with zero.
7. Record reviewer identity only when a real reviewer has performed the work. Record a verification date only when the review happened.
8. Revisit time-sensitive claims when official updates occur and when a correction is reported.

## Game testing records

For a claimed game mechanic or cheat input, document platform, game version/build, exact steps, observed result, test date, limitations, and evidence reference. Do not call a claim tested without performing the test. Do not infer save, achievement or trophy behavior from unrelated games or generic reports.

## Corrections

Use the existing Contact form. Ask the visitor for the affected URL, issue, supporting evidence, and optional email. Keep submissions private. An editor should log receipt, review outcome, correction, and date without promising a response deadline. Significant changes should include a visible correction note where useful.

## AI assistance

AI may help organize, summarize or draft, but it is not a source. An editor must verify claims against opened source material before publication. AI-generated artwork must be identified as illustrative where readers could mistake it for official game imagery or a faithful character depiction.
