# Asset rights register

The authoritative repository inventory is `images/asset-manifest.json`.

| Group | Known method | Current status | Required action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand mark, SVG hero, road and region artwork | Repository-original SVG | Approved for site publication in the manifest | Keep source files and design provenance. |
| Editorial raster images | Owner-supplied, described as illustrative | Pending commercial-rights review in the manifest | Owner must confirm origin, generation terms and commercial-use rights before ads or external licensing. |
| Character, vehicle, location and mission art | Illustrative artwork, not official game capture | Must carry descriptive alt text and not imply a factual game depiction | Retain a caption or nearby label where the distinction matters. |

Do not infer commercial rights from an AI-generation claim alone. Do not claim third-party permission unless documented.
