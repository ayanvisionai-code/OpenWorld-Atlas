Editorial Standards

OpenWorld Atlas is an independent, unofficial information publication. We aim to make the evidence behind a claim clear and to show uncertainty when evidence is missing.

How we research

We start with sources that directly support the claim: Rockstar Games, Take-Two, official platform pages, support documentation, and documented developer statements. Established journalism can be useful for reporting and direct testing. Social posts, leaks, search snippets and AI output are research leads, not evidence by themselves.

Our labels

Officially confirmed

A relevant primary source supports the exact claim.

Independently verified

A documented, reproducible test supports the claim. The published record must identify the method, platform or version, and date.

Reported

A credible source reported the information, but it is not confirmed by an official source or independently reproduced by OpenWorld Atlas.

Unconfirmed, speculation or unknown

These labels mean the evidence does not justify presenting the detail as established fact.

Publication review

A database record is not eligible for public display until an editor records a source, evidence review date and approval. Publication state and evidence status are separate. A published report may still contain clearly labelled reporting; it must not be described as official confirmation.

Corrections

Use the Contact page to report an incorrect fact, broken source, misidentified image or outdated information. Include the page URL, the issue and a supporting source if available. Requests are privately reviewed. We do not promise a response time. Significant corrections may carry a correction note on the affected record.

AI assistance and artwork

AI may assist with drafting, organization or illustrative artwork. It is not an evidence source. Artwork described as illustrative, conceptual or AI-generated is not an official screenshot and should not be read as a confirmed depiction of a game character, vehicle, place or event.

Current review status

Legacy pages and records are under review. We will not claim that every historical page has been verified until that review is complete. See the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy for related information.
