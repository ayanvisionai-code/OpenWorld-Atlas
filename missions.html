GTA 6 Missions & Story

Five chapters, ~91 main missions, ~50–80 hours. Mission profiles below are verified from published walkthroughs; story-sensitive content is blurred until you choose to reveal it.

The five chapters

1
Just Like Paradise — 21–23 missions
2
Paradise Lost — 11–14 missions
3
Tits Ass Drug Money — 26 missions
4
Vice City, Baby — 25–26 missions
5
Top of the World, Ma — 7 missions

Counts differ across walkthroughs (91 vs 96 mains) because of how short mandatory story steps (depositing cash, paying fees) and optional jobs are tallied. The in-game mission list runs past 140 entries. readdork · leonidatricks
