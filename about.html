About OpenWorld Atlas

OpenWorld Atlas is an independent, unofficial gaming information and editorial publication covering Grand Theft Auto VI. We publish source-labelled records, explain uncertainty, and correct material errors rather than filling gaps with guesses.

How we verify information

officially confirmed

Officially confirmed

A directly relevant Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox, or other primary source supports the exact claim.

independently verified

Independently verified

A reproducible test or evidence record documents the platform, version, method and result.

reported

Reported or unconfirmed

Credible reporting is labelled as reporting. Unsupported legacy detail is held for review rather than presented as established fact.

unknown

Unknown or not available

No reliable information is available. We keep that state visible instead of inventing a value.

What we will never do

  • Invent cheat codes, prices, stats, bios, missions or release information.
  • Fill unknown fields with plausible guesses — unknowns stay visibly unknown.
  • Publish unverified "leaks" as news.
  • Present illustrative or AI-generated artwork as official game imagery or a confirmed depiction of a named character.
  • Pretend to be affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Corrections & contact

Found an error or a better source? Tell us via the contact and corrections page. Read the Editorial Standards for the review process and the disclaimer for asset and rights information.
