About OpenWorld Atlas

OpenWorld Atlas is an independent, unofficial gaming information and editorial publication covering Grand Theft Auto VI. We publish source-labelled records, explain uncertainty, and correct material errors rather than filling gaps with guesses.

How we verify information

confirmed

Confirmed

Supported by reliable primary evidence: Rockstar/Take-Two official pages and press releases, official platform pages (PlayStation/Xbox), or direct in-game documentation credited to its publisher.

supported

Supported

Supported by credible independent sources (Polygon, GameSpot, GamesRadar, Metacritic, BBC, VGC, PowerPyx…). Every claim links its named source.

unconfirmed

Unconfirmed

Reported by a named outlet but not sufficiently verified — e.g. the early-2027 PC port report. Always labelled, never blended into facts.

not available

Not available

No reliable information exists yet — like undiscovered cheat inputs or unpublished vehicle stats. We show the empty state honestly instead of inventing a value.

What we will never do

  • Invent cheat codes, prices, stats, bios, missions or release information.
  • Fill unknown fields with plausible guesses — unknowns stay visibly unknown.
  • Publish unverified "leaks" as news.
  • Use official game screenshots or promotional artwork — all imagery here is original.
  • Pretend to be affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Corrections & contact

Found an error or a better source? Tell us via the contact & request page — corrections are prioritized. Full policies live on the disclaimer & credit page.
