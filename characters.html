The question everyone asks: is Lucia trans?

No — and there's no evidence she is. Game Rant reviewed every theory after launch: "Throughout all the marketing for GTA 6, and all the dialogue in-game, Lucia is referred to as a woman by everyone because she is a woman." The prologue places her in a women's-only prison, and the story makes clear she hid her pregnancy from Jason. Rockstar has not commented. The rumor started with a December 2023 viral tweet citing a sourceless Reddit thread.
Sources: Game Rant · NPCWire · Sixth City Map — origin of the rumor

Mandy — the viral Chapter 1 character

Met in "Pipework" (the game's third mission) when Jason collects Brian Heder's rent. She flirts with Jason while he fixes her sink — and the scene's horror details (a severed finger in the sink, acid tubs by a possible fresh grave) made her the talk of launch week. Played by Lexie Shoaibi, voice + motion capture, confirmed by the actress on Instagram. She was never in a trailer.

Sources: Vice · Wikipedia · indy100

The Niko Bellic cameo

⚠️ Mild story spoiler. GTA 4's protagonist appears as an older man in "Taking Liberties" (Chapter 5) — confronting shipping boss Giorgio Gallo over a $2 billion cartel shipment. He mentions he hasn't killed anybody since 2012. Michael Hollick returns as his voice, credited only as "The Hitman" to keep the cameo a surprise.
Sources: PC Gamer · esports.gg · GamesRadar
