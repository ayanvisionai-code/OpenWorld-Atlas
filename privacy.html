Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

Nothing is collected on our servers. This site is static — it does not run accounts, comments, or hidden trackers. The only data stored is in your own browser's local storage, purely to power convenience features:

  • Recent searches — your last few search terms (stored locally, never transmitted). Clear them any time with the "Clear recent" button in the search panel.
  • Content requests you submit — if you use the request form, your question (and optional email, if you provide it) is held in your browser and offered to you for sending by your own email client. It is not published.

2. What we don't do

  • No analytics, cookies or tracking pixels are active.
  • No advertising scripts run. Ad placeholders are visibly disabled.
  • No account registration, no passwords, no payment data.
  • We never ask for console or game-store credentials anywhere on this site.

3. Third-party services

External links (to Rockstar, Take-Two, Metacritic, gaming publications, etc.) take you to third parties governed by their own privacy policies. Loading fonts uses Google Fonts, which receives standard technical request data per its own policy.

4. If monetization is added later

If advertising or analytics are ever enabled, this policy will be updated first, with a consent mechanism where required (e.g., GDPR/CCPA), and ads will remain clearly separated from content.
