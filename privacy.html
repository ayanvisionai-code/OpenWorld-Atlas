Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

When you submit the contact form, the website sends your question, selected category, optional email address and standard request metadata to the configured Supabase backend. Submissions are stored privately for editorial review and are not published. The site also uses browser session storage for a short email-notification throttle and may use local browser storage for convenience features when enabled.

  • Contact and correction requests — collected to review a request, respond if you supplied an email, and maintain editorial corrections.
  • Session-storage values — used for limited in-browser functionality such as notification throttling and draft previews for signed-in editors.

2. What we don't do

  • No AdSense script or publisher identifier is included in this repository. Advertising is not currently described as active.
  • Public visitors do not create site accounts through the public pages. Editorial users sign in through Supabase Authentication.
  • The site does not request console or game-store credentials, payment information, or passwords through public forms.
  • We never ask for console or game-store credentials anywhere on this site.

3. Third-party services

The site uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for configured backend features, and Google Fonts for typography. These providers may process standard technical request data under their own policies. External links take you to websites with their own privacy practices.

4. Audience measurement

We use audience measurement services to understand how visitors read OpenWorld Atlas, improve content navigation, and maintain publication reliability.

  • Vercel Web Analytics — used to count aggregate visits and page views on our public site. Page addresses are reduced to their paths before reporting, so search terms and URL fragments are excluded. Admin pages and editorial previews are excluded. Learn more: How Vercel processes analytics data.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-FEDMCV24K7) — optional audience analytics operated by Google LLC. Under our Google Basic Consent Mode implementation, Google Analytics is strictly disabled by default: no analytics scripts are loaded, no tracking pings or network requests are dispatched, and no analytics cookies are created unless you provide affirmative consent.

When analytics consent is granted, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (such as _ga and _ga_FEDMCV24K7) to measure aggregated visitor volume, clean page paths, browser and device categories, and general interaction trends. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses (IP address data is processed temporarily upon receipt to determine approximate geographical location before being discarded). We do not use Google Signals, advertising personalization, remarketing, or advertising user data features, and we do not transmit form inputs, email addresses, passwords, account data, or search terms to Google Analytics.

You can accept, decline, or withdraw your analytics consent at any time using the Cookie & Analytics Choices link in the footer of any public page, or by clicking the button below. When you withdraw consent, tracking is immediately disabled, accessible analytics cookies are removed, and the denial is remembered.

For additional details regarding Google's data handling practices, see Google Analytics Data Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, use the Contact page and select the appropriate category. The operator must provide a monitored privacy contact and retention period before a commercial launch. See the disclaimer and Editorial Standards.
