GTA 6 on PC, Platforms & Price

The most-asked platform questions, answered flatly: GTA 6 is not on PC, not on PS4, and not playable natively on Xbox One (cloud streaming only). No PC version has been announced. Here's everything verified about where you can play it, what it costs, and when the multiplayer and PC versions are expected.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC? The honest status

What's confirmed

  • GTA 6 is not available on PC as of Nov 25, 2026.
  • Rockstar has not announced a PC version — no date, no price, no store listing, no system requirements.
  • Xbox debunked launch-week PC streaming rumors: Xbox CSO Matthew Ball confirmed GTA 6 would not be an Xbox streaming exclusive or streamable on PC at launch.
  • Take-Two says platform announcements come from its publishing labels — so any real PC news will come from Rockstar, not leaks.

What's reported (unconfirmed)

  • A GamesBeat report (Oct 13, 2026, updated Nov 18) claims Rockstar is targeting the first three months of 2027 for the PC port.
  • This matches Rockstar's historical pattern: GTA 5 launched on consoles Sept 2013 and hit PC in April 2015 (~19 months later).
  • Treat any "PC release date" you see elsewhere as rumor unless Rockstar posts it on rockstargames.com or the Rockstar Newswire.
Bottom line: if you want to play GTA 6 today, you need a PlayStation 5 (or PS5 Pro) or an Xbox Series X|S. For PC, wait for an official Rockstar announcement — the only credible public signal is the GamesBeat early-2027 window, and even that is unconfirmed.
Sources: VGC — GTA 6 PC guide · The Independent — early 2027 report · IBTimes UK · Polygon — Xbox debunks PC streaming

Price & editions

Standard

$79.99

The full game, single-player experience. Digital and physical — note that even physical boxes contain a download code inside, not a disc-based install (Rockstar confirmed this at pre-order).

UK: £69.99.

Ultimate

$99.99

Digital only. Exclusive extras "threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story", uncovered behind each chapter:

  • Jason's safehouse vehicles: Dinka Enduro motorcycle, Crest Kayak, Ganado Retro pickup build
  • Shitzu Squalo fishing boat (Washington Beach dock)
  • '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy + the Paradise Garage (Watson Bay) with weapon locker and fenced-goods storage
  • Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers (Vercetti Estate, palm-tree-etched grips)
  • Personalized Girardi ES9 (Jason) and Klose K17 (Lucia) pistols
  • Exclusive side content: the PTT Youngin$ gang compound raid and the Classic Car Collection restoration missions from fixer Wyman

Bought Standard already? An Ultimate Edition Upgrade costs about $20 on the Xbox Store.

Sources: Take-Two — pre-order & pricing press release · USA Today · The Verge · Kotaku · Engadget

When is GTA 6 Online coming out?

No official date exists. Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 launched as a purely single-player experience — its own press language calls it a "single-player experience set in the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet."
  • History says "later": GTA Online launched ~2 weeks after GTA 5; Red Dead Online launched in beta ~1 month after RDR2 and fully ~1.5 years later.
  • Speculation: a Bank of America investor note (reported by GameSpot, Oct 2026) referenced a potential new GTA Online ("GTA Online 2.0") arriving around Q1 2027. A Twitch executive also suggested 2027 in coverage of the launch window. Neither is confirmation.
  • What's certain: as of Nov 25, 2026, there is no GTA Online for GTA 6, no beta, no announcement. Anything claiming otherwise is speculation or fake.
Sources: The Independent — when is GTA 6 Online coming out · GameSpot — bank note speculation · GamesRadar

Release-day facts people still search

What time did GTA 6 come out?

Midnight local time on November 19, 2026, per PlayStation's official support page — meaning the game rolled out across the world as time zones hit midnight. Digital pre-orders could pre-load from November 12, 2026.

Sources: PlayStation support, Take-Two press release.

Which editions exist?

Two: Standard ($79.99, digital + physical) and Ultimate Edition ($99.99, digital only, with the threaded extras above). There is no season pass or subscription requirement announced for the single-player game.

Sources: USA Today, Take-Two.

Is GTA 6 PS5 Pro Enhanced?

Yes — PlayStation's store page lists GTA VI as PS5 Pro Enhanced.

Sources: PlayStation.com.
