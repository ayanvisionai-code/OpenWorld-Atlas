2. Images and artwork — original by design

Artwork on this site includes original design elements and AI-generated editorial illustrations. Some illustrations are generic or conceptual and do not depict official game assets. We are auditing asset provenance and rights; original or AI-generated status does not guarantee that an asset is free of third-party rights restrictions.

  • No Rockstar/Take-Two logos are used as site branding; the site's name, logo and graphics are our own.
  • Where an official visual exists (e.g. the printable Leonida map, the cover poster), we link to it on Rockstar's official site instead of copying it here.
  • We do not host, distribute or link to any game files, executable software, cracks or unauthorized downloads.
  • Asset provenance and licensing are being reviewed. Do not assume that every asset has documented commercial permission; unresolved assets are listed for owner review.
  • Rights holders: if you believe any element should not appear here, contact us via the email below and we will remove it promptly, before any formal process is required.

3. Information sources & accuracy

We aim to cite sources that directly support each material claim. The site contains legacy content that has not yet been fully reviewed; a source link or verification label should not be taken as proof that every detail was independently checked. See our Editorial Standards and report suspected errors through Contact.

  • We do not present rumors as facts. Where information is unconfirmed (for example, the reported early-2027 GTA 6 PC window), we say so explicitly, name the reporting outlet, and label it "unconfirmed".
  • Time-sensitive figures, dates and prices can change. Pages should identify the source and date checked; where that review has not occurred, the claim remains unresolved.
  • Where two reliable sources disagree (for example, on how mission counts are tallied or on where cheat inputs are entered), we disclose the disagreement rather than picking silently.
  • This site is editorial/informational. Quoted lines from sources are short excerpts used for identification and commentary, with links to the full original articles.

4. Cheat code authenticity policy

Cheat inputs are community-documented, not official Rockstar material. Our labeling rules:

  • Documented input — shown only when an editor has reviewed a claim-specific source and approved the record for public display.
  • No verified input published — no code, unlock condition or effect is represented as established until evidence supports it.
  • We do not assert saving, achievement or trophy effects without direct, claim-specific evidence.
  • Be cautious with third-party downloads or services that request account credentials or payment in exchange for cheat functionality.
  • We review material correction and rights requests through the Contact page. We do not promise an immediate response time.

5. External links

We link to original articles and official pages so readers can read the full context at the source. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content, availability, or policies. Links are provided for attribution and further reading only — no endorsement is implied in either direction.

6. Copyright concerns & takedown contact

If you are a rights holder (or their agent) and believe any content here infringes your rights, please contact us with: (a) identification of the content, (b) the URL on this site, (c) your rights in the material, and (d) your contact information. We will review and remove the material promptly upon verification.

Contact: use the content request form — messages reach us directly and the inbox address is kept private.

We respond to good-faith inquiries first and aim to resolve any concern without formalities.

7. No warranties; gaming account safety

  • The site is provided "as is". We make no warranties about the completeness or fitness of the information for your purposes.
  • Do not enter console account credentials into this site. Advertising is not currently represented as active; this statement should be revisited if advertising is enabled.
  • Cheat usage consequences (e.g. disabled trophies/saves for a session) are documented from our listed sources; always save your game before using them.
