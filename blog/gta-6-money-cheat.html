GTA VI Money Cheat: Research Status

We have removed the earlier claim that a specific GTA VI money cheat gives a fixed amount or uses a known button sequence.

OpenWorld Atlas has not completed direct source review and reproducible testing for that claim. We will not publish a money-cheat amount, input, unlock condition, or saving and trophy effect until the evidence supports each detail.

Current status: unconfirmed. Treat third-party “money cheat” and “generator” claims with caution, especially where they ask for account credentials, payment, downloads, or personal information.
