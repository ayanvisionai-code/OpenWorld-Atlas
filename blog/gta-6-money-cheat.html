The GTA 6 Money Cheat, Fully Explained

Quick answer: the GTA 6 money cheat code is UP, RIGHT, DOWN, LEFT, UP, LEFT, DOWN, RIGHT on the D-pad — entered with the in-game phone open, holding L1/LB and having pressed R1/RB. One input gives you $250,000. It only works after you finish the main story, and using it disables saving and trophies for that session. (Source: PowerPyx, corroborated by Polygon, Vice and Insider Gaming.)

The code, exactly

UpRightDownLeft UpLeftDownRight

On PS5: press Up on the D-pad to open the phone → hold L1 and press R1 → enter the arrows. On Xbox Series X|S: identical arrows, but hold LB and press RB. The full step-by-step with the unlock prerequisites is on our complete cheat code database.

Three rules that make the money cheat different from every other GTA

  1. You must finish the story first. GTA 6 locks all cheats behind story completion. On an unfinished save the code simply does nothing — this is the single most common reason people report "the money cheat doesn't work".
  2. Each input pays once. The cheat gives a flat $250,000 per entry. There is no unlimited-money variant anywhere in the game's 61-cheat list — the complete PowerPyx database confirms it, and every major outlet's list agrees.
  3. Cheats pause your real progress. Entering it (or any cheat) disables saving and trophies for the rest of that session. It's designed as a toy for after the credits, not an economy tool.

"GTA 6 unlimited money cheat" — why every claim is fake

Searches for unlimited money in GTA 6 spiked massively at launch — and so did the scams. The facts:

  • The game's complete cheat list (61 cheats across five categories) contains exactly one money cheat. No cash multiplier, no infinite money, no spawn-money item.
  • Websites that claim to "generate" GTA 6 money, YouTube videos pushing "cheat installers", and APKs promising cash are scams — many are malware or account-phishing. GTA 6 has no PC version yet, so anything offering a "GTA 6 PC money cheat download" is fake by definition.
  • Legitimate cheats are built into the game, cost nothing, and never require installing anything or entering account details.

Making money legitimately during the story

Since the cheat needs a finished save, in-story wealth comes from the real economy: fenced contraband (which also pre-counts toward the money cheat's unlock), mission payouts, vehicle sales (sell $500,000 in vehicles and you even unlock a screen-filter cheat), collectible sales, and side jobs.
