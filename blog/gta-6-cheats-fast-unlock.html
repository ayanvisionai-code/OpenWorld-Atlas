GTA VI Cheat Unlock Claims: Research Status

We have withdrawn the earlier claims about a fixed number of cheats, story-completion requirements, challenge totals, and fast unlock routes.

These are separate gameplay claims and require evidence for each exact mechanic. OpenWorld Atlas has not yet documented independent gameplay testing or reviewed sources that directly substantiate them.

Current status: unconfirmed. This page will be updated only with claim-specific evidence and an editorial review date.
