Unlocking GTA 6's Cheats, Fast

Quick answer: four cheats unlock the instant you finish the story — $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. Everything else is unlocked by challenges: complete 5 challenges in a category for one vehicle spawn, all 10 for the second; shooting-range medals unlock ammo cheats; and doing partner activities (holding hands, store robberies) unlocks screen filters. (Sources: PowerPyx, TheGTA6Map.)

The challenge system — how the other ~50 open up

  • 5 challenges in a category → one vehicle-spawn cheat (e.g. 5 fitness challenges → Tuff BMX; 5 shopping → Maibatsu Boomazoom).
  • All 10 in a category → the second, bigger vehicle (all survival → Rhino Tank; all shopping → Truffade Thrax; all challenges total → the Hauners P-996 Lazer fighter jet).
  • Shooting ranges → the ammo trio: 25 bronze medals → Max Ammo, 25 silver → Infinite Ammo, 25 gold → No Reload.
  • Discover vehicles in the Progress menu → Ambulance, MTL Pumper, HVY Bulldozer spawns.
  • Weird and wonderful grinds → 50 cocaine flamingos (Inflatable Flamingo), 100 fish (Fisheye filter), 50 meditations (Fast Forward), 6 minutes at 6 stars (Migraine), 100 stolen cop cars (Hellscape).

Partner activities unlock the best filters

Three of the 16 screen filters come from playing as a couple: Dollhouse (spend 8 hours with your partner — input known), Faded Glory (rob 50 stores together — input known), and Golden Hour (4 hours holding hands — input not yet discovered). It's the slowest grind in the game and the community's favorite.

Fastest route for cheaters in a hurry

  1. Finish the story → grab your 4 instant cheats (incl. the $250,000).
  2. Grind shooting-range medals → Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo, No Reload.
  3. Do 100 workouts → Body Armor.
  4. Pick your favorite vehicle category and clear its 5 challenges → your first spawn cheat.
  5. Save before each cheat session — remember, cheating pauses saving and trophies for that session.
