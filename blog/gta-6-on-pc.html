GTA 6 on PC — the Honest Answer

Quick answer: GTA 6 is not on PC, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version — no date, no price, no system requirements. The only credible public signal is a GamesBeat report claiming Rockstar targets the first three months of 2027 — unconfirmed. (Sources: VGC, The Independent.)

What's verified vs what's reported

Verified
  • GTA 6 released Nov 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.
  • No PC listing exists on any official storefront.
  • Xbox's CSO publicly debunked launch-day PC streaming claims.
  • Take-Two says platform announcements come from its publishing labels — i.e., Rockstar itself.
Reported (unconfirmed)
  • GamesBeat (Oct 13, updated Nov 18, 2026): Rockstar targeting Q1 2027 for the PC port.
  • Historical precedent: GTA 5 launched Sept 2013 on consoles and reached PC in April 2015 (~19 months later).
  • No leak of system requirements has ever been verified — any "GTA 6 PC requirements" page today is speculation.

The scam wave around "GTA 6 PC download"

Because no PC version exists, every site, torrent or "installer" claiming to offer GTA 6 on PC is fake by definition — typically distributing malware, adware or credential-stealing fake launchers. The safe options today are exactly two: a PS5/PS5 Pro, or an Xbox Series X|S (Xbox One players can stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming). When the real PC version is announced, it will appear on the official Rockstar VI site first.

What PC players can reasonably expect

If the GamesBeat window holds, a PC release in early 2027 would put the gap at roughly 4–5 months — unusually fast for Rockstar, whose past console-to-PC gaps ran about 19 months (GTA 5). Whether it's 2027 or later, the same principle applies: wait for the official announcement, don't pre-order from unofficial stores, and be skeptical of "system requirements" until Rockstar publishes them.
