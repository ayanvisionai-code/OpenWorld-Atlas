How to Enter GTA 6 Cheat Codes, Step by Step

Quick answer: press Up on the D-pad to open the in-game phone → hold L1 (PS5) / LB (Xbox) and press R1 (RB) → enter the cheat's arrow sequence on the D-pad. Codes only work after finishing the main story, and only if that cheat's unlock condition is complete. (Sources: PowerPyx, Vice, Insider Gaming.)

The full method

  1. Beat the story. GTA 6's cheats are locked until the credits roll. The Cheats List menu appears under Progress Menu → Cheats only after story completion — it doesn't exist during the story.
  2. Check your unlock. In the Cheats List, each cheat shows either its code (unlocked) or its unlock requirement. Inputs do nothing for cheats you haven't unlocked.
  3. Open the phone. Press Up on the D-pad.
  4. Hold the modifier: hold L1 on PS5 / LB on Xbox, then press R1 / RB.
  5. Enter the arrows on the D-pad while still holding — e.g. Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right for the $250,000 money cheat. The arrow sequences are identical on both consoles.

Why your cheat code isn't working — the 4 real reasons

  • The save hasn't finished the story. The most common cause by far. All 61 cheats are story-locked.
  • The modifier wasn't held. Entering arrows with the phone open but L1/LB not held does nothing.
  • The cheat wasn't unlocked yet. Each cheat has its own challenge (25 shooting-range medals for Max Ammo, 100 workouts for Body Armor, etc.).
  • You copied a made-up code. Most circulating lists contain unverified inputs. Use our database, which attributes every known input to its source and marks the rest "not yet discovered".

The cost of cheating (know before you enter)

Using any cheat disables trophies/achievements and saving for the remainder of that play session. Recommended routine: save first, play with cheats as long as you like, then quit without saving — your pre-cheat save stays intact, and trophies can start unlocking again in your next session.
