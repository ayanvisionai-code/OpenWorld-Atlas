How GTA VI Cheat Claims Are Reviewed

We have removed earlier instructions that described a specific cheat-entry method, controller sequence, story prerequisite, and game consequences.

OpenWorld Atlas will publish an input only after it has a claim-specific source and, for independently verified status, a documented platform, version, method, result and test date. We do not treat a repeated list, search snippet or old GTA code as enough evidence.
